THE HALL OF FAME

This league has gone on long enough where there have been both highlights and lowlights. From draft picks to trades, these are the selections that truly left a legacy for the league.

Greatest League Moment: The Six Team Megatrade

The standout moment in league history, the massive six-team blockbuster. Originally constructed as a way for Matt to try and get Ausar Thompson from Anthony, more and more teams got added until every team got pulled in. The trade ended up as follows:

  • Matt:
    • Receives: Zach Edey, Ausar Thompson, Isaiah Jackson, Jalen Hood-Schifino
    • Gives up: Moses Moody, Dalton Knecht, Ron Holland, Taylor Hendricks, 2024 Winter R2 Swap with Mitchell
  • Anthony:
    • Receives: Sam's 2025 Summer R2, Dlaton Knecht, Ron Holland, Taylor Hendricks, Anthony Black
    • Gives up: Ausar Thompson
  • Michael:
    • Receives: Jeremy Sochan, Yves Missi, Precious Achiuwa
    • Gives up: Kyle Filipowski, Bilal Coulibaly, Jaden McDaniels, Baylor Scheierman
  • Mitchell:
    • Receives: Moses Moody, Jaden McDaniels, Bilal Coulibaly, 2024 Winter R2 Swap with Matt, AJ Griffin
    • Gives up: Precious Achiuwa, Anthony Black, Yves Missi, Jalen Hood-Schifino, Josh Primo, Zach Edey, Isaiah Jackson
  • Sam:
    • Receives: Kyle Filipowski, Baylor Scheierman
    • Gives up: 2025 Summer R2
  • Mr. L:
    • Receives: Josh Primo
    • Gives up: AJ Griffin

Greatest Draft Steals:

There are some all time draft steals that have happened in this league. Here are some of the highlights:

Anthony selects Victor Wembanyama R1P6 of the Summer 2021 Draft

You're not going to find any other round one draft picks here. But Anthony got the best fantasy player in the NBA after every other owner passed on him. He was first and he was right. Picks like this are what makes Anthony competitive despite the misplays.

Michael selects Herb Jones R2P1 of the Winter 2021 Draft

The first great active player picked in the expansion draft. Herb Jones is a standout, putting up great stock numbers with good shooting. Expansion seconds almost never turn out, but this was one of the times it did.

Mr. L selects Trey Murphy R5P1 of the Summer 2021 Draft

Another pick early in league history, this pick was awarded due to the passing of Terrence Clarke. It turned into something great: someone who Yahoo ranked as the 17th best player in the 2025-26 season. Considering the third best player in the prior two rounds was Day'Ron Sharpe, this was a complete steal.
