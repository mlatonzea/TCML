About The Owners

The Terrence Clarke Memorial League is the product of a long-time local friendship. Running out of Neenah, Wisconsin, five friends (and one dad) teamed up to make the wackiest fantasy leagues possible. After a couple attempts, this dynasty league is the one that stuck.

Sam, Injury Hell

The most recent champion, Sam has one goal with his draftees: superstardom or bust. While he has some major misses like James Bouknight and Johnny Davis, he made some home run selections like Franz Wagner, Cam Boozer, and Chet Holmgren. Current star: Cade Cunningham.

Anthony, Big boys don't kawhi

The first league champion and by far the most volatile drafter. He drafts people you've never even heard of. Sometimes it works, like when he picked Victor Wembanyama after EVERYONE passed on him in 2021. Other times, he picks Gus Yalden. You truly never know. Current star: Victor Wembanyama.

Michael, Duren Duren

One time champion and runner-up of the most recent season, Michael's team has ups and downs. This year he said he was actively tanking before going on a run in the playoffs. His team is full of "tank commanders", including Josh Giddey, Jonathan Kuminga, and Ace Bailey. Current star: Jalen Duren

Mr. L, Pokemon Max

The original dynasty. With sutds like Tyrese Maxey, Trey Murphy III, Evan Mobley, and Deni Avdija, this team is always the favorite at the beginning of the season. Even though he's coming off a down year of getting 4th place, he has studs like Tyran Stokes coming up in future drafts. Current star: Anthony Edwards.

Matt, Pritchard Fan Club

Though he got 7th place last year, this is one team you shouldn't sleep on. With Brandon Miller, every Pacer you can think of, and the combination of Jalens Williams and Johnson, this is a team that can win any given week. Current star: Tyrese Haliburton.

Mitchell, GG's Smoke and VORP Shop

The only team that hasn't won a championship, but arguably the deepest team. The combination of Maxime Raynaud, Jabari Smith, GG Jackson, Isaiah Stewart, and Onyeka Okongwu give him the deepest center rotation by far. Not to mention Current star: Alperen Sengun.
