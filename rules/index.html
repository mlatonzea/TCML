League Rules

Need a rundown of the rules of the league? You came to the right spot.

Draft Rules

The draft rules are somewhat convoluted, especially as they have evolved over time. The following rules are the current rules, not the historical rules.

Summer Draft

  • The Summer draft is three rounds of six picks.
  • The draft is in the inverse order of the end of year standings.
  • The draft is non-serpentine. All rounds have the same order.
  • Any player who has played in a regular season or postseason NBA game is ineligible for the draft.
  • Any player who has been drafted and subsequently cut is ineligible for the draft.
  • Picks can be appended onto the end of the draft for any reason.

Winter Draft

  • The Winter draft is two rounds of six picks.
  • The draft is in the inverse order of the standings at the time of the draft.
  • The draft is serpentine, so the order of the recond round is the reversal of the first.
  • Players who have played in NBA games but have not exhausted their rookie limits are eligible for the draft.

Roster Rules

  • Teams are allowed up to 23 players.
  • The scoring positions are as follows:
    • PG
    • SG
    • G x2
    • SF
    • PF
    • F x2
    • C x2
    • F/C
    • Util x4
  • Incomplete rosters are allowed.

Scoring Rules

Starting in 2026, the TCML is a 10 cat league. The following 9 categories are in play every year:
  • Points
  • Rebounds
  • Assists
  • Three Pointers Made
  • Field Goal Percentage
  • Free Throw Percentage
  • Steals
  • Blocks
  • Turnovers (lower wins)
In addition to that, a 10th category will be decided by the lowest placing team the prior year. The category for the 2026 season is Double-Doubles.

Playoffs

Six teams make the playoffs, which are decided by regular season record. The top two teams get a first round bye.
